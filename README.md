## What's new

- **Ads are easier to read:** business and campaign cards have a brighter glass-style look, with menus and text that are easier to follow.
- **Preview your own ad:** see your own video while it is being reviewed. Previewing does not add views or likes.
- **Clearer campaign results:** colorful charts make views and likes easier to follow.
- **Wallet activity at a glance:** a colorful chart compares coins you purchase, receive and send, and animates when you open your wallet.

All 24 app languages and existing features are still included.
